UASFLA / Yellow Book Valuation Workflow 📖 UASFLA Standards Guide ↗

Primary Appraisal (UASFLA) 2 CFR §200.311 ↗

Why this is required: Federal funds cannot pay more than Fair Market Value (FMV). Yellow Book (UASFLA) rules are stricter than commercial appraisals and require explicit identification of federal intended users, valuation date compliance, and post-storm market impact adjustments.

Technical Review Appraisal UASFLA Section C ↗

Why this is required: Federal standards mandate an independent technical review appraiser to formally evaluate and approve or disapprove the primary appraiser's methodology before grant funds are released for purchase.
💡 Onboarding Note for New Staff — Appraisal Rules
  • • 12-Month Expiration: Appraisals are generally valid for 12 months. If closing is delayed, an appraisal update or recertification letter is required prior to closing.
  • • Storm Event Clause: In coastal areas, appraisals must include a statement evaluating whether recent hurricanes or storm surge altered the land's value or topography.
Title Commitment, NOFI & Subsurface Mineral Rights 📖 RESTORE Rules ↗

Mineral Remoteness Report Surface Mining Standard ↗

Why this is required: If mineral rights are severed (owned by a third party), a geologist or legal report must prove that the likelihood of surface mining or disruption is "so remote as to be negligible" to protect conservation investments.

Notice of Federal Interest (NOFI) 2 CFR §200.311(c) ↗

Why this is required: Ensures the public's investment is protected in perpetuity. A draft NOFI containing mandatory RESTORE Council language must be reviewed by legal (`Jana`) and recorded in local land records at closing.
💡 Onboarding Note for New Staff — Title & Mineral Reviews
  • • Surface Leases: Outstanding oil/gas surface leases or pipeline easements must be reviewed by Legal to confirm they do not impair the conservation purpose.
  • • NOFI Recording: Closing cannot be finalized without a commitment from the title company to record the NOFI alongside the Warranty Deed.
Uniform Relocation Act (URA) & Voluntary Sale Verification 📖 FHWA URA Portal ↗

Willing Seller Letter 49 CFR §24.101 ↗

Why this is required: Proves the acquisition is voluntary. The letter must state that the landowner was informed of the appraised Fair Market Value and that the agency will not use eminent domain if negotiations fail.

Uniform Relocation Act (URA) Compliance 49 CFR Part 24 ↗

Why this is required: Federal law protects residential or commercial tenants from illegal displacement. Even if the sale is on the open market and URA benefits do not apply, an explicit URA acknowledgment letter is required for the grant file.
💡 Onboarding Note for New Staff — URA Requirements
  • • Open Market Exception: If property was publicly advertised for sale, eminent domain does not apply, but the Willing Seller Letter must still disclose FMV.
  • • Tenant Protections: Always verify if any agricultural, hunting, or residential leases exist on the land before closing.
Boundary Survey, GIS Spatial Files & Stewardship Plan 📖 FGDC GIS Standards ↗

Boundary Survey & Canal Acreage Survey Specs ↗

Why this is required: Verified by GIS specialist (`CiCi`) to confirm boundary corners, spatial shapefiles, and legal acreage (including water/canal features) match the approved grant workplan.

Conservation Management Plan RESTORE Science Standard ↗

Why this is required: Requires Program Science Lead (`Heather Young`) approval to ensure long-term ecological stewardship, invasive species control, and habitat management goals are met.
💡 Onboarding Note for New Staff — GIS & Science Reviews
  • • GIS Deliverables: Shapefiles or GeoJSON boundary files must be verified in GIS before post-acquisition desk review closeout.
  • • Environmental Site Assessment (Phase I ESA): Checks for hazardous materials/RECs under ASTM E1527 standards.
Post-Closing Deliverables & Financial Desk Review 📖 SF-429 Instructions ↗

Recorded Deed & Recorded NOFI 2 CFR §200.311 ↗

Why this is required: Verifies that the warranty deed and recorded NOFI are formally entered into public county land records showing federal interest restrictions.

ASAP Drawdown Reconciliation & SF-429 SF-429 Form Set ↗

Why this is required: Reconciles final settlement statement (closing costs & land purchase price) against grant disbursements in ASAP before final desk review approval.
💡 Onboarding Note for New Staff — Desk Review Closeout
  • • SF-429 Attachment A: Standard Form 429 must be submitted for all real property acquired with federal grant funds.
  • • Improper Payment Checks: Ensures closing cost line items (title fees, legal costs, surveys) align with federal cost principles in 2 CFR 200 Subpart E.
